WHAT IT DOES

Where everything lives

  • Overview what to do now: the plan, the briefing, your queue and the command line.
  • Agenda when: today’s plan and calendar, training days, and your energy check-in.
  • Workbench doing: the full queue, quick capture, posting reminders and important mail.
  • Performance how it is going: streak, where your effort went, the weekly objective and your numbers.
  • Plan my day builds real time blocks around your calendar and energy. Tap a block to start a focus session on it.
  • The life cards judge each area — slipping, stalled, moving — and filter the queue when tapped.
  • The command line works offline: add finish the essay by friday 5pm, what is due, plan my day. Type help for the full list.

CONNECTED APPS

Link PocketAthlete

Two tokens, minted inside PocketAthlete. They stay in this browser, and re-minting either one in PocketAthlete revokes the old value immediately.

The training feed token is the ?token= value in the calendar subscription URL PocketAthlete gives you. The health push token is the ?t= value in your Apple Health upload link. Reading training needs Google sync connected too, because PocketAthlete’s calendar feed is built for calendar apps and a browser is not allowed to read it directly.
